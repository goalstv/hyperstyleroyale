## Cost drivers worth watching

1. **Music licensing** is the largest line no software decision can reduce. It scales with carriage
   type and audience.
2. **Index source licences** are the second. Each additional signal has a contract behind it, and
   the methodology page publishes which ones are not yet signed.
3. **CDN egress** scales directly with success and is the line that surprises media companies most.
4. **Original production** is the differentiator and the expense. A cheaper network is a video
   jukebox, and a video jukebox does not get carriage.

## What is deliberately not in these numbers

No cable carriage fee revenue. No guaranteed FAST revenue share. No archive revenue before
chain-of-title clearance. No measurement-dependent CPMs before measurement is bought.
