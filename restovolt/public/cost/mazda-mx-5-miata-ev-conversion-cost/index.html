What drives the cost of a Miata conversion

Conversion considerations

RESTOVOLT keeps the original five-speed gearbox and power-plant frame. The motor bolts to the gearbox through an adapter plate and coupler, and the motor mounts use the original engine-mount locations. Retaining the power-plant frame preserves the drivetrain stiffness and shift quality the car is known for.

Batteries split across a box in the engine bay ahead of and above the motor, and a box in the trunk and spare-tire well. Keeping the top of the front box below the hood line is the tightest constraint in the design; check hood-latch clearance during dry fit.

Weight and packaging notes

The original engine and fuel system are light, so the weight budget is small. Most builds use a 24 or 32 kWh pack; 40 kWh is the packaging ceiling for current box designs and will need spring and damper changes. Keeping mass low and between the axles protects the handling balance that makes the car worth converting.

Line items people forget

  • Brake upgrades sized for the new weight and torque
  • Springs and dampers re-rated for the battery mass
  • DC-DC converter, 12 V battery relocation and gauge integration
  • Coolant loop, pump and radiator for the inverter and motor
  • Heating and defrost, if the car will be driven year-round
  • HV commissioning tests, labeling and the documentation file for insurance and registration

Miata conversion cost FAQs

How much does a Miata EV conversion cost?

Sample planning bands put complete drivetrain parts for a 1990–1997 Mazda MX-5 Miata between $21,000 and $37,000, plus 180–260 hours of shop labor. Pack size and whether you keep the gearbox drive most of the difference.

What is the cheapest way to electrify a Miata?

Keep the original transmission with an adapter plate, choose tested salvage battery modules sized for your real driving, and use the verified Miata Core Fitment Pack rather than custom-designing mounts.

How much range will an electric Miata have?

Typical builds see 80–150 miles at about 210 Wh per mile, depending on pack size, tires and speed.

Does the electric Miata keep its manual gearbox?

Yes. The Fitment Pack adapter plate mates the motor to the original five-speed, and the power-plant frame stays in place.
