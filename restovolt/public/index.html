How it works

From teardown to road test, without re-engineering the basics.

01

Choose your platform

Open a vehicle hub for packaging notes, difficulty, range and cost bands, and builds that have already been through the shop.

02

Configure the build

Set goals for range, performance, budget and reversibility. The configurator checks motor, inverter, battery and BMS compatibility as you go.

03

Download and fabricate

Get STEP and STL files, a bill of materials, torque specs and an assembly checklist. Print the non-structural parts in-house and send structural parts to a fabrication partner.

Safety and engineering standards

High voltage is not a place for guesswork.

Every pack carries an engineering review date and a field-proven build count. Structural parts are always labeled for machining or metal printing, and structural downloads require terms acknowledgment.

Read our safety and compliance standards

High-voltage safety. EV conversions involve lethal DC voltages. Installation and commissioning must be performed or supervised by a qualified, HV-trained technician following our safety standards.
