About

Classic soul. Electric heart. Engineered to fit.

Classic EV conversions are slow and expensive because every shop re-engineers the same mounts, adapter plates and battery box layouts from scratch, and sources parts from a dozen fragmented places.

RESTOVOLT replaces that guesswork with a verified, vehicle-specific fitment library and a streamlined parts path. We start with 3D scan data, engineer the parts that every conversion of a platform needs, and refine each design with field reports from the shops that build them.

What we believe

  • Safety is visible, not buried in a PDF.
  • Designs should be honest about what they are: structural parts are labeled, reviewed and dated.
  • Shops should own their customer relationships. We route leads; we do not compete for builds.
  • Classic cars deserve reversible options.

RESTOVOLT is independent and not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer. Model names are used only to identify the vehicles our designs fit.
