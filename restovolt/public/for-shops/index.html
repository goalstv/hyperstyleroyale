For shops

Add EV conversions without becoming a CAD shop

RESTOVOLT replaces weeks of re-engineering with verified, vehicle-specific designs and a streamlined parts path, so your team spends its hours building, not measuring.

See plansTry the configurator

Fitment Packs

Motor mounts, adapter plates and battery boxes for each platform, with STEP and STL files, BOM, torque specs and checklists. Structural and non-structural parts are labeled for the right manufacturing method.

Configurator and quotes

Recommend a compatible drivetrain in minutes, catch mismatched components before you buy them, and export a quote with your logo.

Shop dashboard

Track every project through Teardown, Fabrication, Install, HV Commissioning, Road Test and Delivered. Manage saved builds, purchased packs, leads and team seats.

Lead routing

Owners researching conversions are matched to certified shops near them that build their platform. Pay per lead or include it in your plan.

Certified Installer badge

Complete HV training and a documented commissioning audit to earn the badge on your directory profile and in routed leads.

Scan upload Phase 2

Scan a vehicle we do not cover yet. If accepted, you earn contributor credit and a revenue share on every pack sold. Learn more
