Assembly checklist

  1. Confirm HV isolation: pack disconnected, service plug removed, verify 0 V at the inverter DC link.
  2. Dry-fit every structural part before final welding or torque. Check clearances to brake lines and fuel/vent remnants.
  3. Torque structural fasteners to spec in the pack sequence, then witness-mark them.
  4. Route HV cables through the supplied guides; maintain at least 25 mm from exhaust-heat or sharp edges.
  5. Install fusing and the manual service disconnect in the locations shown on the routing guide.
  6. Run insulation-resistance and pre-charge checks before first energize (HV Commissioning stage).
  7. Re-check torque after the first 50 road miles.
