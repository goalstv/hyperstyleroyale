# FOUNDER TYCOON — Progression Loop Design

> ⚠️ **Superseded.** This was the initial loop sketch. It has been absorbed and
> greatly expanded into the flagship creative bible —
> **[`FOUNDER_SIMULATOR.md`](FOUNDER_SIMULATOR.md)**. Start there. This page is
> kept only as the short "loop mechanics" reference.

> **Working title:** Junior Tycoon
> **Pitch line:** *"The first Founder Tycoon"* — the world's first playable
> success story.

A design brief for a life-progression game in the **GOALS** universe. It borrows
the *feel* of a city tycoon — constant, visible forward motion — but replaces the
core currency of **money** with **opportunity**, and replaces "own the biggest
city" with "build a life, a network, and a legacy."

This is a companion concept to [HYPERSTYLE ROYALE](../README.md): same brand
philosophy (**GOALS**), different medium and loop. Where HYPERSTYLE ROYALE is a
linear narrative with an *awakening* meter, Founder Tycoon is an open
progression sandbox with a *reputation* meter.

---

## Why this works

City tycoon games are addictive because the player *always feels like they're
progressing*:

- Bigger city
- Better buildings
- More money
- New areas
- More status

We keep that psychological loop intact and re-skin every rung of it around a
founder's journey instead of a landlord's. The dopamine mechanic is proven; the
theme is what differentiates us.

**The core substitution:** money → **opportunity**. You don't get addicted to a
balance going up. You get addicted to *doors opening*.

---

## The player fantasy

You don't start rich. You start with almost nothing and a big dream.

**Starting inventory**

| Item | Role |
|------|------|
| **$40** | Your entire runway. Every early choice is a trade-off against it. |
| **Backpack** | Carries what little you own; upgrade slot later. |
| **Notebook** | Where GOALS and ideas are tracked (diegetic UI). |
| **MetroCard** | Movement — the city is gated by what you can afford to reach. |
| **One internship** | Your first income + your first relationship node. |
| **One big dream** | The long arc; expressed as your three starting GOALS. |

Your first objective is **not** buying a mansion. It's **surviving your first
week in New York.**

---

## Progression arc

The loop escalates from survival → building → influence. The "tycoon" you become
is measured in **influence**, not cash.

### Early game — *survive the first week*
- Earn your **$40 stipend**
- **Buy food, or save it?** (the first meaningful trade-off)
- Ride the **E train**
- Print **flyers**
- **Build relationships**
- Unlock your **first mentor**
- Produce your **first mixtape**

### Mid game — *build the foundation*
- Better **apartment**
- **Studio access**
- Internship **promotions**
- New **neighborhoods**
- Better **laptop**
- **Camera**
- **Business cards**
- **Networking events**

### Late game — *build the legacy*
- Creative **agency**
- **Music label**
- **Media company**
- **AI startup**
- Build **GOALS**
- Buy your **first home**
- **Mentor new players**

> The endgame flips the loop outward: instead of accumulating, you start
> *investing in other players* — mentoring becomes the highest-status activity.

---

## The map is the progression

New York (and the road to it) *is* the tech tree. You start with a single
unlocked area and open the rest by **completing story objectives**, not by simply
paying currency. Geography = narrative gating.

```
🟢 Fort Lauderdale     ← start here
        ↓
🔒 Gainesville
        ↓
🔒 JFK Airport
        ↓
🔒 Jamaica, Queens
        ↓
🔒 Baisley Park
        ↓
🔒 Manhattan
        ↓
🔒 Los Angeles
        ↓
🔒 The Garage          ← the legacy endgame
```

Each node is a **chapter of the life**, not a real-estate purchase. Unlocking
Manhattan should *feel* earned — a story beat, not a transaction.

---

## Founder Reputation (the real level system)

The thing city tycoons *don't* have. Instead of only earning money, you level up
a **founder identity**. Reputation is the score that actually matters; money is
just one input to it.

| Level | Title |
|------:|-------|
| 1 | **Hustler** |
| 2 | **Creator** |
| 3 | **Intern** |
| 4 | **Strategist** |
| 5 | **Creative Director** |
| 6 | **Executive** |
| 7 | **Founder** |
| 8 | **Mentor** |
| 9 | **Legend** |

Each title should gate real capabilities (studio access, hiring, opening a new
node) so the ladder is *functional*, not cosmetic — and each should re-theme the
HUD/vibe the way HYPERSTYLE ROYALE's grade shifts cold→warm with awakening.

---

## The GOALS mechanic

The signature system — it hard-wires the brand philosophy into the loop instead
of chasing currency.

**On join, every player sets three personal GOALS.** Examples:

- Make **$10,000**
- Release your **first song**
- Meet **five mentors**
- **Graduate**
- **Build a company**

As a player accomplishes a GOAL, they gain **XP** and **unlock a story chapter**.
GOALS are the connective tissue between the free-form sandbox and the authored
narrative: the map unlocks the *where*, GOALS unlock the *why*.

**Design intent:** money is a means; GOALS are the ends. The scoreboard the
player checks is their three GOALS, not their bank balance.

---

## Positioning

Don't pitch this as a tycoon game — that puts it in a crowded shelf of
restaurant/mall/city sims.

Pitch it as:

> **"The first Founder Tycoon."**
>
> or
>
> **"The world's first playable success story."**

The premise itself is the differentiator: the player isn't running a business,
they're **building a life, a network, and eventually a legacy.**

## Open questions / next steps

- **Platform:** the loop and area-gating read as a **Roblox** experience; confirm
  target before committing to systems design.
- **Multiplayer surface:** the mentoring endgame implies other players — is this
  shared-world (mentor real juniors) or solo with NPC juniors?
- **Economy tuning:** define the exact opportunity/reputation/XP curves so early
  survival stays tense and late-game investment stays rewarding.
- **Node content depth:** each of the 8 map nodes needs its own objective set,
  cast, and unlock condition — the equivalent of HYPERSTYLE ROYALE's per-episode
  chapters.
- **GOALS catalog:** author the full menu of selectable starting GOALS and map
  each to the story chapter it unlocks.
